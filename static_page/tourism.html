Red Soil
The Red Soil (locally known as the Pilipil Red Soil) in Cervantes, Ilocos Sur, is a striking, Mars-like natural attraction that has become a popular off-the-beaten-path destination for hikers and eco-tourists.
Bessang Pass Monument
The Bessang Pass Natural Monument is a protected area and historical site located in Cervantes, Ilocos Sur, Philippines. It commemorates one of the most significant victories of World War II.
Gambang Falls
A majestic cascading waterfall offering icy cold mountain waters perfect for nature adventurers and swimming enthusiasts.
Nakayang View Deck
Offers a stunning panoramic view of the entire valley of Cervantes and the winding mountain highways.

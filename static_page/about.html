About Cervantes

Discover the Summer Capital of Ilocos Sur

Our History

Cervantes holds a profound place in heritage, acting as a strategic historical mountain pass bridging the coastal regions to the grand Cordillera ranges. It is widely celebrated as the home of the historic Battle of Bessang Pass fought during World War II, serving as a monument to the bravery and resilience of our local heroes.

Our Vision

To establish Cervantes as a premier eco-tourism destination and progressive agro-industrial hub in northern Luzon, driven by transparent, inclusive, and adaptive governance.

Our Mission

To uplift the quality of life for every citizen by promoting resilient agricultural programs, safeguarding environmental biodiversity, and preserving cultural identity.
